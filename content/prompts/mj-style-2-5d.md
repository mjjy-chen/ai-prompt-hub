**2.5D** — MidJourney V5 风格关键词

**分类**: 维度 / Dimensionality

**效果示例**:

![2.5D style example](https://gh-proxy.com/https://raw.githubusercontent.com/willwulfken/MidJourney-Styles-and-Keywords-Reference/main/Images/MJ_V5/V5_Alpha_1/Midjourney_Styles/2.5D.webp)
